2. What the app is

BClear Lab is a tool for practising British English pronunciation on your own. It reads the movement of your lips through the camera and shows you what to change. It is not a course or a qualification, it doesn’t certify your level of English, and it doesn’t replace a speech therapist or medical advice.

The beta is free. There are no paid features in it.
